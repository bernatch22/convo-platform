# ms-19 — console polish: the number where you look, the reservations you can read

**Where it came from.** Two operator complaints minutes after a deploy: the
project's phone number had vanished from the project page (a previous fix
moved it to Pipeline, correctly, and forgot where people look), and the Board
showed counters when the operator wanted the reservations — who, when, with
whom.

**The number.** One component, two frames: `PhoneLines` renders as a panel in
Pipeline and as a header strip in Talk, with a single code path for the row
and the formatting, so the two screens cannot disagree. `readable()` groups
E.164 the way a person reads it aloud and refuses to guess unknown prefixes.
A hook, not a route loader, on purpose: Talk is the lazy route, and a loader
would hold the whole conversation behind a round trip for a number nobody is
waiting on.

**The reservations.** The counters read the log, whose summaries are
PII-filtered by design — that stays. The reservations themselves live in the
BUSINESS system, and the console is who business data is for. The route goes
api.py → `core/registry` (the sanctioned door onto tenants) → a new
console-only adapter capability, `list_records`. The design decision that
scales: **one capability, not two** — the adapter answers with its own
`shape`, its own column labels and its own word for state; the only field the
console interprets is `tone`. The clinic gets an agenda, the shop gets orders
with an order's own Spanish statuses, and a tenant declaring nothing gets an
honest sentence naming the capability it lacks. A test pins the contract with
a shape the codebase has never heard of (`kennels`), proving the console
learned nothing about clinics.

Two pieces the work forced into existence: the **ledger**
(`core/adapters/ledger.py`, write-through only) — adapters are built fresh
per session in memory and api.py is another process, so without it a booked
cita was invisible by construction; and the join key — the appointment **id**
crosses the PII mask because an id is not a person, landing verbatim in
`result_summary` while the name stays masked.

**Recorded, not built:** `cancel_slot` stays `side_effect: write` (inside a
move it is compensable — that is what write means); a standalone cancel would
be irreversible and get its consent pair. Tienda's specs declare no
`result_summary`, so its rows cannot yet join to a session — one line per
spec when it matters.

**Verify it live:** open a project — the number is under the title; make a
booking by phone and open Board — the cita is there with name, hour,
professional and its session.
